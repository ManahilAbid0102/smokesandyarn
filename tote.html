Sunshine Granny Tote

Materials Needed

  • 100% Cotton Yarn in pastel yellow, pink, and white
  • 4.0mm crochet hook (Size G)
  • Tapestry needle
  • Fabric for lining (optional)

Instructions (Granny Square)

Make 13 squares total. Join them using the invisible seam method.

Round 1 (Yellow): Magic ring, ch 3 (counts as dc). 2 dc in ring, ch 2. [3 dc in ring, ch 2] 3 times. Sl st to top of ch 3. Fasten off.

Round 2 (Pink): Attach yarn in any ch 2 space. Ch 3, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc in same space (corner made). *Ch 1, [3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc] in next ch 2 space. Rep from * around. Sl st to top of ch 3. Fasten off.

Round 3 (White): Attach yarn in any corner space. Ch 3, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc in same space. *Ch 1, 3 dc in next ch 1 space, ch 1. [3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc] in next corner space. Rep from * around. Sl st to top of ch 3. Fasten off.
