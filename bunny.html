Mint Bunny Pattern

Materials Needed

  • Pastel mint green and pastel pink yarn (DK weight)
  • 3.0mm crochet hook
  • Safety eyes (8mm)
  • Fiberfill stuffing
  • Yarn needle

Instructions (Head)

R1: 6 sc in magic ring (6)

R2: inc in each st around (12)

R3: [sc in next st, inc] 6 times (18)

R4: [sc in next 2 sts, inc] 6 times (24)

R5-R10: sc in each st around (24)

(Add safety eyes between R7 and R8. Stuff firmly before closing.)
