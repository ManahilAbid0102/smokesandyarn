Lavender Dreams Blanket

Materials Needed

  • Worsted weight yarn in lavender, peach, and baby blue
  • 5.0mm crochet hook (Size H)
  • Tapestry needle
  • Scissors

Instructions (Granny Stripe Pattern)

Foundation: Ch a multiple of 3 + 2 (e.g., 122).

Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. Turn.

Row 2: Ch 3 (counts as dc), dc in same st. *Skip 2 sts, 3 dc in next st. Rep from * across, ending with 2 dc in last st. Turn.

Row 3: Ch 3, 3 dc in space between the 2 dc and 3 dc group from previous row. 3 dc in each space across, ending with 1 dc in top of turning ch. Turn.

(Alternate colors every 4 rows to create the beautiful striped pastel effect!)
